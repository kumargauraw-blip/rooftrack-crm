# Storm / door-knocker campaign — social copy

All variants keep the same two-beat message: **don't sign anything** → **free honest look, 817-809-7000**.
The warning is what gets shared. The offer is what converts. Lead with the warning.

---

## 1. Facebook — main organic post

This is the one built to be shared, not to sell. Post it as-is from the business page.

> **ROOFERS DRIVING YOU NUTS?**
>
> If you've had three knocks on your door this week, you're not alone. The storm came through and so did every out-of-town crew with a clipboard.
>
> Here's the only advice that matters, and it's free:
>
> **DON'T SIGN ANYTHING AT YOUR DOOR.**
>
> Not a contract. Not an estimate. Especially not the form that's "just so we can take a look" or "just to file with your insurance." Those are often contingency agreements or an assignment of your insurance benefits — sign one and you may have handed that company your roof before anyone's been up there. Getting back out of it is a fight.
>
> You don't have to sign a thing to find out if your roof is damaged.
>
> **Want an honest inspection? Call or text 817-809-7000.**
> 100% free. No signatures. No contract. No pressure.
> We'll tell you the truth — including "your roof is fine," if that's the answer.
>
> We're local. We've been here. We'll be here after these guys are gone.
>
> 🔗 HonestRoof.com
>
> *Share this with a neighbor. Somebody on your street is about to sign something they'll regret.*

**Why the last line:** an explicit share instruction roughly doubles share rate versus hoping people do it on their own. It's also true, which is why it doesn't read as a growth hack.

## 2. Nextdoor — post here before Facebook

Nextdoor is the better platform for this exact message: hyper-local, and door-knocker complaints are already the most active thread type on it after a storm. Search your neighborhood feed for existing "who are these roofers" threads and reply there too — that's warmer than a cold post.

> **Anyone else getting hammered by roofing door-knockers this week?**
>
> Since the storm we've had a lot of neighbors call us rattled about it, so — quick PSA, and I'm not trying to drum up work with this part:
>
> **Don't sign anything handed to you at your door.** Not even the "this is just so we can inspect it" form. A lot of those are contingency agreements or an assignment of your insurance benefits, and signing one can lock you into that company before anyone has looked at your roof.
>
> Ask for the company's Texas address. Ask who'll be here in two years for the warranty. If they push you to sign right now, that's your answer.
>
> If you do want to know whether you actually have damage, we'll come look for free — no signature, no contract, no obligation. Call or text 817-809-7000. And if your roof is fine, we'll tell you that, and leave.
>
> — Dennis, HonestRoof.com

**Tone note:** Nextdoor punishes anything that reads like an ad. The PSA has to genuinely stand on its own — a neighbor could act on it and never call you, and it still did its job. That's what makes the last paragraph acceptable there.

## 3. Boosted ad / short paid version

Keep paid copy under ~125 characters before the "See more" fold.

> **DON'T SIGN ANYTHING A DOOR-KNOCKER HANDS YOU.**
>
> Want an honest look at your roof instead? 100% free, no signature, no contract. We'll tell you the truth.
>
> 📱 Call or text **817-809-7000**
> 🔗 HonestRoof.com

**Targeting:** homeowners, 10–15 mi around the hardest-hit ZIPs. You already know which ones — pull the top cities out of the CRM (Arlington, Fort Worth, Grand Prairie, Mansfield lead your customer base) and weight toward wherever the storm actually tracked.

**Suppression list:** exclude your existing customers from paid targeting. They're getting the email, and paying to reach them twice wastes budget.

## 4. Graphic / yard sign / truck magnet

Your original line, tightened. This is already good copy — the caps and the bluntness *are* the brand, so keep them.

```

DON'T SIGN ANYTHING!!

WANT AN HONEST INSPECTION?
CALL OR TEXT 817-809-7000

100% FREE · NO SIGNATURES REQUIRED
```

Set "DON'T SIGN ANYTHING" as the largest element. It's the line that stops someone, and it's the only line that has to be readable from a moving car.

## 5. SMS to past customers (optional, separate from email)

Only to customers who've texted you before or explicitly opted in — cold bulk SMS carries real TCPA exposure, unlike email. Keep it short and always include a way out.

> Dennis at HonestRoof. Lot of door-knockers out since the storm — don't sign anything they hand you, even the "just to inspect" form. Want a free honest look at your roof? Call or text 817-809-7000. Reply STOP to opt out.

---

## Sequencing

1. **Nextdoor + Facebook first**, today. The door-knocking is happening right now; this message has a shelf life of about two weeks.
2. **Email to past customers** same day or next.
3. **Boosted ad** after you see which organic post gets traction — boost the winner rather than guessing.
4. Re-share the Facebook post once about 5 days later. Storm-chaser fatigue peaks *after* the first wave, when people are worn down and more likely to cave.
